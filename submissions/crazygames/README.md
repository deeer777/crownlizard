## Preview videos

Record the two previews from the exact unpacked CrazyGames package, never from crownlizard.com. Follow `preview-shot-list.md`. Keep the footage silent, 15–20 seconds long, free from the mouse cursor, black bars, social icons and promotional text. The first frame should be the matching static cover.

The videos are deliberately not synthesized from still images: portal visitors must see honest gameplay and the controls must match the uploaded build.
