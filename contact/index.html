SECURITY REPORTS

SEND A SECURITY REPORT

Describe the issue without accessing another player’s data or disrupting the service.

WHAT TO INCLUDE

  • Your callsign, if the issue concerns a registered player.
  • The device and browser you used.
  • What you expected, what happened and the approximate time.
  • A screenshot when it helps explain the issue.

Never send your password, verification link, recovery token or authentication code. Crown Lizard support will not ask for them.
