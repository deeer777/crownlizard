PILOT TRAINING

HOW TO SURVIVE AN ENDLESS RUN

Stay mobile, dash through danger and build one weapon toward its final form. Crown Lizard rewards controlled movement more than frantic tapping.

MOVE

Drag anywhere on mobile. Use WASD or the arrow keys on desktop.

DASH

Tap the DASH control or press Space. Dashing briefly protects the ship and escapes crowded lanes.

AUTO FIRE

Your active weapon fires automatically. Positioning and upgrade choices are your job.

WEAPONS AND UPGRADES

Weapon crates introduce Blaster, Spread, Pulse, Laser and Tesla. Repeated crates improve the current weapon up to MK5. A fully upgraded weapon unlocks a choice between two final mastery paths after a Warden victory. The paths are mutually exclusive for that run.

CROWN POWERS

Defeat a zone Warden to choose one of three run-long powers. Some improve mobility or recovery; cursed choices grant larger advantages while raising enemy pressure. The best build depends on your weapon and the dangers ahead.

GAME MODES

  • Chill: a gentler pressure curve for learning weapons and movement.
  • Arcade: the intended competitive balance.
  • Crowned: faster and less forgiving, with its own global leaderboard.

GLOBAL WARDEN

Signed-in pilots can equip an unlocked Crown Armory blueprint and enter a 90-second Boss Assault. Verified damage contributes to a shared event health bar. Arsenal Rank adds a capped bonus, while diminishing returns keep repeated attempts useful without letting one player dominate forever.

QUICK SURVIVAL TIPS

  1. Save at least one dash charge for overlapping enemy attacks.
  2. Do not chase every pickup through a dangerous formation.
  3. Master one weapon instead of spreading upgrades too thin.
  4. Watch the stage bar: the Warden is your next build decision.
