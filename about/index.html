THE GAME

CHASE THE CROWN. KEEP THE RUN ALIVE.

Crown Lizard is a free endless arcade shooter where movement, timing and build choices matter more than purchased power.

BUILT FOR QUICK RUNS AND DEEP MASTERY

Every run begins with a standard weapon and grows into a specialised build. Dash through enemy formations, collect weapon crates, defeat Wardens and choose permanent Crown Powers for that run. The difficulty keeps cycling upward, so the real finish line is the global high score.

ENDLESS RUNS

Four distinct zones repeat with stronger enemies, elite formations and tougher Wardens.

TEN FINAL FORMS

Five weapon families each branch into one of two mutually exclusive mastery paths.

COSMETICS ONLY

Ships, crates and Crown Store rewards never increase ranked damage or survivability.

A LIVING ARCADE GAME

Crown Lizard is actively developed as a mobile-first Progressive Web App. It supports touch, keyboard and desktop play, persistent player accounts, public Pilot Files, server-owned high scores and cooperative Global Warden events.
