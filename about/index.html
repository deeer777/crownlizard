THE GAME

CHASE THE CROWN. KEEP THE RUN ALIVE.

Crown Lizard is a free browser arcade shooter where pixel-art space combat, movement, timing and build choices matter more than purchased power. Play instantly on mobile or desktop with no download.

BUILT FOR QUICK RUNS AND DEEP MASTERY

Every endless survival run begins with a standard weapon and grows into a specialised build. Dash through enemy formations, collect weapon crates, defeat Wardens and choose permanent Crown Powers for that run. The difficulty keeps cycling upward, so the real finish line is the global high score.

ENDLESS RUNS

Four distinct zones repeat with stronger enemies, elite formations and tougher Wardens.

TEN FINAL FORMS

Five weapon families each branch into one of two mutually exclusive mastery paths.

COSMETICS ONLY

Ships, crates and Crown Store rewards never increase ranked damage or survivability.

A LIVING ONLINE ARCADE GAME

Crown Lizard is actively developed as a mobile-first Progressive Web App. The online shoot ’em up supports touch, keyboard and desktop play, persistent player accounts, public Pilot Files, server-owned high scores and cooperative Global Warden events.
