# Crown Foundry — future crownlizard.com retention system

Status: product decision saved for later. Do not include this system in the CrazyGames lane.

## Locked direction

- Crown Foundry is a small idle-adjacent metagame, not an AFK resource faucet.
- Active, qualified runs provide the input material required to start a job.
- Jobs may run for 2, 4 or 8 hours while the player is away.
- Rewards are cosmetic fragments, trails, dash effects or duplicate salvage.
- Jobs never grant score, boss damage, weapon power, lives or ranking advantages.
- Start with one job slot and at most one unclaimed reward.
- All timestamps and claims must be server-owned, idempotent and immune to client-clock changes.
- No real-money purchases. The Crown economy remains ad- and shard-driven.

## When to revisit

Revisit for crownlizard.com after the CrazyGames Basic Launch data is available and the current rewarded-ad work is complete. Instrument job start, return and claim before judging retention impact.
